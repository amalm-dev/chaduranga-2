📖 Piece Manual

♚
King

Moves 1 square any direction. Must be protected — checkmate loses the game.

♛
Queen

Unlimited range — horizontal, vertical, and diagonal.

♜
Rook

Unlimited range — horizontal and vertical only. Can castle with King.

♝
Bishop

Unlimited range — diagonal only. Stays on its starting color.

♞
Knight

L-shape: 2+1 squares. Can jump over other pieces.

♟
Pawn

Moves 1 forward (2 from start). Captures diagonally. Promotes on last rank.

🐅
Tiger — The Hunter

Moves diagonally only if it can capture an adjacent piece (horizontal/vertical) along its path. Jumps over own pieces. Moves to the captured piece's square.

🌲 Forest: capture range +2 squares (once per entry)

🐓
Rooster — The Sentinel

Moves 2 squares diagonally forward. Captures any piece directly in front. Cannot move backward. Jumps over own pieces.

🌊 Water: movement restricted to 1 square for 3 turns

🛕
